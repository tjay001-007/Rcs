 LEGEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   |  v  ^        forward / vertical signal flow
   <==== / ==>    state-&-sensor feedback bus (runs up the right side)
   +==== box      a controller stage or law block
   +.... box      the PLANT (dashed) — physics, not controller
   nu             desired angular acceleration (INDI virtual input)
   G              control-effectiveness matrix (scheduled from aero tables,
                  x live q-bar);  G^-1 = inner-loop inversion gain
   omega_dot_meas measured/filtered body angular acceleration (the INDI feedback)
   q-bar          dynamic pressure 0.5*rho*V^2  — the master scheduling variable

 READING THE CASCADE (outer = slower, inner = faster):
   SEQUENCER   decides the phase & which law owns pitch/throttle   (supervisory)
   GUIDANCE    turns the phase into gamma/V/h/psi references        (trajectory)
   OUTER LAWS  TECS/ACAH/gamma/L1 turn references into RATE cmds    (~1 Hz band)
   ENVELOPE    clamps those commands to alpha / Nz / bank limits    (safety shell)
   INDI        turns rate error into surface INCREMENTS via G^-1    (100 Hz inner)
   ACTUATORS   move elevator/aileron/rudder (+ RCS in vacuum)
   PLANT       aero+prop+gear -> forces/moments -> 6-DOF EOM -> states
   FEEDBACK    states sensed and returned to every stage above
